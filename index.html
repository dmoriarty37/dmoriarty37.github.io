Can a frozen world grow an ocean? 4.5 billion years of Pluto in 15 seconds.

You're looking at a slice through Pluto. It starts frozen solid at 40 K (−233 °C). Radioactive elements in the rocky core slowly release heat, which has to escape outward through the ice. My C++ model splits the planet into 200 shells and steps heat between them over time; when ice hits 273 K it melts, absorbing latent heat and conducting much faster as water. Every frame is real output from the code.
